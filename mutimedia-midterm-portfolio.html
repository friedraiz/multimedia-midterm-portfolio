ABOUT / THE DESIGN JOURNEY

How this portfolio came together

This portfolio compiles every midterm activity from the course — starting with the welcome pubmat and ending with the final GIMP poster. Laid out in order, it reads like a progress bar: shapes first, then shading, then brand thinking, then full composites. What I'm proudest of isn't any single output, it's that the later files took me half the time and twice the confidence of the first ones.
